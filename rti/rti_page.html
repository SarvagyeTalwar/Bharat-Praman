Step 1: Choose Mode
File online at rtionline.gov.in or print the PDF for offline Speed Post.
Step 2: Pay Rs. 10/-
Pay via UPI/cards online or buy a Rs. 10 Indian Postal Order (IPO) at any Post Office.
Step 3: Track 30 Days
Under Section 7(1), the PIO must reply or provide documents within 30 days.
Step 4: First Appeal
If delayed or refused, file First Appeal under Section 19(1) with the appellate authority.
