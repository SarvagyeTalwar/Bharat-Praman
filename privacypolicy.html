Privacy & Data Practices
BHARAT PRAMAN
Overview

Bharat Praman is an open-source political knowledge graph tool built for research and public transparency. We are committed to keeping your experience private and your data minimal.

■ Data Sources

All political data indexed by Bharat Praman — including MP/MLA records, constituency details, ministerial portfolios, and electoral metadata — is sourced from MyNeta.info and other publicly available government and election commission records. No private or classified information is stored or served.

■ Information We Do Not Collect

  • ✓ No user accounts, passwords, or personal identifiers are required or stored.
  • ✓ No cookies or tracking pixels are placed on your device.
  • ✓ No personal data such as name, email, phone number, or location is collected.

■ Query & Usage Data

Search queries submitted to the Bharat Praman knowledge graph may be logged anonymously for the sole purpose of improving query performance and graph accuracy. These logs contain no personally identifiable information and are not shared with any third party.

■ Third-Party Services

This landing page loads resources from third-party CDNs (Tailwind CSS, Google Fonts, React, Framer Motion) which are governed by their own privacy policies. Bharat Praman itself does not integrate with any third-party analytics, advertising, or data-brokering services.

■ Open Source Transparency

Bharat Praman is released under the MIT License. The source code is publicly auditable on GitHub. You are free to inspect exactly what data the system processes and how.

■ Contact

For any privacy-related questions or concerns, please reach out via the project's GitHub repository.
