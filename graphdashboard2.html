Why Neo4j GraphRAG beats standard Vector RAG (ChromaDB / LangChain)
Standard Vector RAG searches text embeddings in isolation and suffers from semantic blindness: it cannot traverse from a PDF budget line-item to the politician responsible for it. Bharat Praman links 44,453 PDF chunks directly into a 104,000+ relationship Neo4j Knowledge Graph, achieving single-query multi-hop traversal with zero hallucination.
